Course Flow

Besisahar→Manang 3,540 m→Thorong La 5,416 m→Muktinath→Pokhara

Elevation Profile

The world-class long circuit around the Annapurna massif. After two acclimatization days in Manang, cross Thorong La (5,416 m), visit Muktinath, and return to Pokhara — ecology, culture, and religion in one trek.

Route by Section

Days 1–5
Besisahar → Manang
~60 km 5 days
⌄

Follow the Marsyangdi valley through forest, fields, and desert-alpine terrain.

Road sections can be replaced by vehicles to shorten the schedule.
Days 6–7
Manang acclimatization
— 2 days
⌄

Complete acclimatization hikes to Praken Gompa or Ice Lake.

Rest-day hikes dramatically raise your Thorong La success rate.
Days 8–9
Manang → Thorong Phedi
~12 km 2 days
⌄

Via Yak Kharka to High Camp (4,925 m) for the night.

It is a high-altitude night — severe headache means postponing the pass.
Day 10
Cross Thorong La 5,416 m
~16 km 7–9 h
⌄

Pre-dawn start over the pass (5,416 m) and a long descent to Muktinath — the summit of the circuit.

Cross before noon — afternoon wind and snow are dangerous.
Days 11–14
Muktinath → Pokhara
~60 km 4 days
⌄

Visit Muktinath temple, soak at Tatopani hot springs, and return to Pokhara.

Dull road sections can be replaced by jeep or bus.

Gear

  • Sleeping bag
  • Full insulated set
  • Dust mask
  • Power bank

Permits & costs

  • ACAP ~NPR 3,000
  • Packages $800–2,500
  • Guide ~$21/day (NTB rules)

Cautions

  • Cross the pass before noon
  • Altitude sickness
  • Knee strain on descents

Course Flow

Manang→Tilicho Base Camp→Tilicho Lake 4,919 m→Thorong La→Pokhara

Elevation Profile

The circuit's crown-jewel extension to Tilicho Lake (4,919 m) from Manang — with camping near 5,000 m, this suits only fully acclimatized trekkers.

Route by Section

Days 1–5
Besisahar → Manang
~60 km 5 days
⌄

Climb the standard approach to Manang with ample acclimatization.

Tilicho trekkers should push the Manang acclimatization hikes harder.
Days 6–7
Manang → Tilicho Base Camp
~12 km 2 days
⌄

Via Khangsar to Tilicho Base Camp (4,150 m) — a high camp.

If altitude symptoms worsen at the camp, skip the lake climb the next day.
Day 8
Tilicho Lake 4,919 m
~3 km RT 4–5 h
⌄

Explore the lake shore or the viewpoint toward Tilicho Pass (5,315 m) with views of the Great Barrier.

The lake sits at 4,919 m — minimize exertion and stay hydrated.
Day 9+
Resume circuit and descend
~80 km 6–8 days
⌄

Cross Thorong La, visit Muktinath and Tatopani, and return to Pokhara.

After this extension, vehicle replacement of road sections is a reasonable choice.

Gear

  • Winter sleeping bag
  • Camping gear (check lodge options)
  • Pulse oximeter
  • High-calorie food

Permits & booking

  • Same ACAP permit
  • Limited teahouses on the Tilicho section
  • Guide mandatory

Cautions

  • Camping near 4,900 m
  • Descend immediately in bad weather
  • Have an evacuation plan
